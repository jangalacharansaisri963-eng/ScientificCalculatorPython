# Physics Formulas Reference

This document outlines key formulas available in the `ScientificCalculator.functions.physics` module.

## 1. Kinematics and Mechanics
- **Kinetic Energy**: $E_k = \frac{1}{2} m v^2$ (in Joules)
- **Gravitational Potential Energy**: $E_p = m g h$
- **Newton's 2nd Law**: $F = m a$
- **Universal Gravitation**: $F = G \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}$ where $G \approx 6.6743 \times 10^{-11} \text{ N m}^2/\text{kg}^2$
- **Escape Velocity**: $v_e = \sqrt{\frac{2 G M}{r}}$

## 2. Electromagnetism
- **Coulomb's Law**: $F = \frac{1}{4 \pi \varepsilon_0} \frac{|q_1 q_2|}{r^2}$
- **Ohm's Law**: $V = I R$
- **Electrical Power**: $P = V I = I^2 R = \frac{V^2}{R}$

## 3. Thermodynamics and Modern Physics
- **Ideal Gas Law**: $P V = n R T$
- **Photon Energy**: $E = h f$
- **Mass-Energy Equivalence**: $E = m c^2$
- **Lorentz Factor**: $\gamma = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - v^2/c^2}}$
